What is Thyroid Eye Disease?

Thyroid Eye Disease (TED) is an autoimmune condition that affects the muscles and tissues around the eyes. It is closely associated with thyroid disorders — particularly Graves' disease — and can cause significant changes in vision, comfort, and appearance.

Early identification of symptoms is critical. This tool guides you through a clinically-inspired series of questions to assess your risk profile and recommend next steps.
